# Rocky Trail

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/rocky_trail
Author: Amal Kumar. License: CC0 (Poly Haven).
Downloaded September 22, 2026 through https://api.polyhaven.com/files/rocky_trail.

`quarry-trail-color.webp` and `quarry-trail-normal.webp` are WebP encodings of
the original 2K diffuse and OpenGL normal maps. Full 2048 × 2048 dimensions
are retained. Normal-map channels are loaded as linear data, not sRGB color.
The game applies its quarry palette and blends the material into terrain.
