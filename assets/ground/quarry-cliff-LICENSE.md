# Rock Face 03

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/rock_face_03
Authors: Dario Barresi (photography), Rico Cilliers (processing).
License: CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license

The official 2K diffuse and OpenGL normal maps were encoded as WebP, with
1K derivatives for low and medium quality. Runtime projection uses world-space
coordinates along each cliff face. The normal maps remain in linear color space.
