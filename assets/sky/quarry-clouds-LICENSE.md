# Quarry 03 photographic sky

Source: https://polyhaven.com/a/quarry_03
License: CC0, https://polyhaven.com/license

The official 8192 x 4096 tonemapped panorama was resized and encoded as 4K
and 2K WebP assets. It supplies the sky above the generated distant ridge.
This is LDR photographic imagery; it is not a radiometrically calibrated HDR
lighting source. The assembled sky is captured into the game's PMREM.
